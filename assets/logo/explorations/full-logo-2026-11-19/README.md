# Full logo explorations (Nov 19, 2026) — in progress, not in use

The R mark (bars turned on their side as the R's stem) built into the wordmark: "RESPEC LABS" where the first R is the mark.
Dan liked layout A (R mark is the R in RESPEC) and B (A + slogan); C (tile + wordmark) and D (stacked) are companions.

Grid: the R's top stroke = gold bar; the R's bowl, the P's loop, the B's middle stroke and both E middle arms all end on the middle blue bar.

Six serif/foot versions (see all-six-versions.png):
1. Slanted serif + curved foot
2. 45° serif + curved foot
3. 45° serif + 45° foot
4. Slanted serif + matching slanted foot
5. E-angle (~10°, the typeface's native angle) serif + foot (5b: shorter serif ending at the stem line)
6. Like 4, with every angled letter end (E, S, C, L) re-cut to the serif angle

Open points from the critique: pick one angle system (5 matches the typeface and the A; 6 doesn't change the A),
choose the foot for large sizes (it vanishes small), darken the light version's orange to ~#C46F00 for contrast.

source/build2.py regenerates the SVGs (needs svgpathtools).
